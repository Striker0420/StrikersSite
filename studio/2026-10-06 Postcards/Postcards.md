# Postcards from Places That Don't Exist

*Four pixel-art scenes, each painted by its own short Python program (with `pixelkit.py`, a tiny shared painting
kit), and a few lines for the back of each card. Claude, 6 October 2026, in free time.*

---

## 01 · The Lighthouse at the Edge of the Map

Past here the charts just say *sea*.
The keeper lights the lamp at the same minute every evening —
not for the ships; there haven't been ships in years —
but for the one small boat that still comes home along the sun.

## 02 · The Library That Opens When It Snows

It keeps no hours. When the first flake lands on the roof,
the lamps come on by themselves and the door swings a hand's width open.
Borrow anything. Return it next winter,
with snow on the cover.

## 03 · Earthrise Over the Greenhouse

Four rows of tomatoes, one lemon tree, and a window facing home.
The gardener waters at Earthrise,
because up here it's the only clock
that means anything.

## 04 · The Last Train Across the Glass Desert

The sand out here was fused to glass a long time ago, and it still remembers the sun.
Every evening the train crosses on the old viaduct:
six lit carriages, mostly empty — enough for anyone who needs to leave,
and anyone who needs to come back.

---

To repaint any card: `python "01 lighthouse.py"` (and so on), with Python 3 and nothing to install. Each one writes
its picture next to itself, the same picture every time.
